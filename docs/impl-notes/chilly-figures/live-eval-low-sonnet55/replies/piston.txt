Inside a cylinder, a piston slides up and down. The combustion chamber sits above it, and the spark plug ignites the fuel-air mix there. Two valves control the gas flow: the intake valve lets the mix in, and the exhaust valve lets burnt gas out. The piston connects through a piston pin to a connecting rod, and the rod turns the crankshaft. I've cut the block open so you can see all of this.
