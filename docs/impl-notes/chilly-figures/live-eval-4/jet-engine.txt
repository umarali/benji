I'll show you the main components inside a jet engine.

A jet engine has several key sections working together: at the front, the fan and compressor stages draw in and compress air. The compressed air enters the combustion chamber where it mixes with fuel and ignites. The hot, expanding gases then rush through the turbine stages, which extract energy to power the compressor and fan. Finally, the exhaust shoots out the back, creating thrust.
