I'll show you a plane in flight with its key aerodynamic parts.

An airplane flies by generating lift from air flowing over its wings. The wings are shaped so air moves faster over the top than the bottom, creating lower pressure above and higher pressure below, which pushes the plane upward. The engines provide thrust to move forward, the tail controls pitch and yaw, and the ailerons on the wings control roll.
