I'll show you what's inside a jet engine with a 3D cutaway model.

A jet engine works by compressing air, mixing it with fuel and igniting it, then expelling the hot gases to create thrust. The main components from front to back are: the intake fan that draws air in, multiple stages of compressor blades that squeeze the air, the combustion chamber where fuel burns, turbine blades that extract energy to power the compressors, and the exhaust nozzle that accelerates the gases out the back.
