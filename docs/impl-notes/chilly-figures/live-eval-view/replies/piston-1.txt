A car engine cylinder contains several key components that work together to generate power:

The **piston** moves up and down inside the cylinder bore. Above it is the **combustion chamber** where the air-fuel mixture ignites. The **piston rings** seal against the cylinder walls to contain pressure and control oil. A **connecting rod** links the piston to the crankshaft below.

At the top, **valves** (intake and exhaust) open and close to let fuel-air mixture in and exhaust gases out. A **spark plug** protrudes into the combustion chamber to ignite the mixture. The cylinder walls are part of the **engine block**, and the top is sealed by the **cylinder head** with a gasket between them.
