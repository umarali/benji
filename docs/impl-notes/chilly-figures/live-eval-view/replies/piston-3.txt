A car engine cylinder contains several key components that work together to convert fuel into motion:

The **cylinder bore** is a precisely machined tube in the engine block. Inside it, a **piston** moves up and down, connected to the crankshaft by a **connecting rod**. At the top, the **cylinder head** seals the cylinder and holds the **valves** (intake and exhaust) that let air-fuel mixture in and exhaust gases out. The **spark plug** ignites the compressed mixture. Between the piston and head is the **combustion chamber** where the fuel-air mixture burns. Piston **rings** seal against the cylinder wall, and the **crankshaft** at the bottom converts the piston's linear motion into rotation.
