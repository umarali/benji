A wind turbine is a tall tower with a nacelle (housing) at the top that holds the generator and mechanical parts. Three long, slender blades are mounted on a hub at the front of the nacelle, rotating to catch the wind. The tower is typically white or light gray, and the whole structure stands on a wide base anchored to the ground.
