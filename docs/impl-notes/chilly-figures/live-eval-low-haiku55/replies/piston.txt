Inside each cylinder is a smooth, round bore where a **piston** slides up and down. The piston's top, together with the cylinder walls and the **cylinder head**, forms the combustion chamber, where fuel and air are compressed and burned. The **spark plug** ignites the mixture, and two or more **valves** let air in and exhaust out. A **connecting rod** links the piston to the crankshaft, turning the up-and-down motion into rotation.
