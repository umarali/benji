# Code of Conduct

This is a short code of conduct adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), which is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is shortened, not the full text; where it
is silent, the Covenant's intent applies.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in Benji, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways
that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good faith.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission. This includes pasting other people's messages, files or API keys into issues.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and
when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and
appropriate action in response to behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
That can include removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions,
and temporarily or permanently banning a person. Reasons are given when it is safe to give them.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at **aliumar.me@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy
and security of the reporter are respected. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer follows the Covenant's ladder: a **correction** (a private written note, a public apology
may be requested), a **warning** (consequences for continued behaviour, including no contact with the
people involved for a period), a **temporary ban** from the project's spaces, and a **permanent ban** for
a pattern of violations or harassment of a person or group.
