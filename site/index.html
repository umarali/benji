A helping hand in your Mac's notch.

Talk to it or type. It looks at your screen, points at what you need and gets things done. And it asks before anything risky.

Early days. It's been used on one Mac, and there's no download yet, so you build it yourself. Here's what's been tested.

What it does

  • Talk or type

    Hold Right ⌥ and talk, or just type. Your voice is turned into text on your Mac.

  • Sees your screen

    It points at the button you meant. If you let it, it clicks, scrolls and types for you while you watch.

    Clicking: not tested live yet

  • Draws it in 3D

    Ask how something works and it builds a labelled model you can spin around, then talks you through it.

  • Does the work

    Runs commands, reads and writes files, searches the web, and drives your apps with AppleScript and Shortcuts.

  • Works in the background

    Hand off a longer job and carry on. It works in its own folder and checks with you when it needs to.

    Experimental

  • From your phone

    Send it a task from your phone over your own Tailscale network. Follow along, stop it, or say yes from there.

    Experimental · setup

It asks first.

The rules are in the code, not in a prompt the model could be talked out of.

  • Before it deletes, sends, buys, or writes outside its own folder, it asks you.
  • Once it has read a web page, every change asks. Whatever the page says.
  • Your keys, mail, messages and browser data stay shut unless you say yes.
  • No account, no tracking, no server of ours. Requests go to the model you pick.
How the rules work

Bring your own model.

Claude by default. Or OpenAI, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi or Qwen, or a model on your own Mac through Ollama or LM Studio. Only Claude has been used for real so far.

Build it yourself.

You'll need macOS 14 or later, Xcode 26, Python 3.11 and a model key.

The setup guide 
$ git clone https://github.com/umarali/benji.git
$ cd benji/companion
$ ./build.sh && open Benji.app

Questions

What leaves my Mac?

Your messages, what its tools find, and screenshots (on by default, you can turn them off) go to the model you chose. Your voice becomes text on your Mac. Nothing comes to us. The details.

Is it safe to let it act?

It's not a sandbox. The rules above stop the risky stuff, but read what you approve.

Will it work on my Mac?

It needs macOS 14 or later. So far it's been used on one Apple silicon MacBook with a notch. Other Macs haven't been tried much yet.

Can I help?

Yes, please. Try building it and tell us where you got stuck. Issues and pull requests are welcome.
