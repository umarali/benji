I'll show you what's inside a car engine cylinder with a cutaway 3D view.

A car engine cylinder contains several key components working together. The cylinder itself is a precisely machined chamber in the engine block. Inside, a piston moves up and down, connected to the crankshaft by a connecting rod. At the top is the cylinder head with valves (intake and exhaust) that open and close to let the air-fuel mixture in and exhaust gases out. Spark plugs ignite the mixture. The piston has rings that seal against the cylinder wall to contain compression and combustion pressure.
