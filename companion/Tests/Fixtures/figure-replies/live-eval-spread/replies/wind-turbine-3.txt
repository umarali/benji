A wind turbine is a tall tower with three long, narrow blades mounted on a hub at the top. The blades rotate to capture wind energy and connect to a nacelle (the housing that contains the generator). The tower is typically cylindrical and tapers slightly toward the top, standing on a wide base foundation.
