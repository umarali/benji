I'll show you what's inside a car engine cylinder with a 3D cutaway view.

A car engine cylinder contains several key components that work together to convert fuel into motion. The cylinder itself is a precisely machined chamber where combustion occurs. Inside you'll find:

The **piston** moves up and down inside the cylinder bore, connected to the crankshaft by the connecting rod. At the top of the cylinder is the **combustion chamber** where the air-fuel mixture ignites. The **cylinder head** seals the top and houses the valves—intake valves let the fuel-air mixture in, and exhaust valves let burned gases out. **Spark plugs** ignite the mixture. The cylinder walls are lined and lubricated, with **piston rings** creating a tight seal to contain combustion pressure and control oil.
