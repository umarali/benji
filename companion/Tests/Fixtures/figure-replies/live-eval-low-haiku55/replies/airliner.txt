A plane flies because its wings push air down, and the air pushes the wings up. The wing's curved top and tilted angle make air move faster over the top, lowering pressure there, and the wing deflects air downward. The reaction force is **lift**. Engines provide **thrust** to overcome drag, and in steady level flight lift balances **weight**.
