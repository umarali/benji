A wind turbine is a tall structure with a tower supporting a nacelle (housing) at the top, which contains the generator. Three long blades extend from the hub in front of the nacelle, rotating to capture wind energy. The blades are typically white or light gray and can span over 100 meters in diameter on large turbines.
