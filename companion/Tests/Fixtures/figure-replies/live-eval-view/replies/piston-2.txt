A car engine cylinder contains several key components that work together to convert fuel into motion:

The **piston** moves up and down inside the cylinder bore. Above it is the **combustion chamber** where the fuel-air mixture ignites. The **cylinder head** sits on top, holding the **intake and exhaust valves** that control when air/fuel enters and exhaust leaves. A **spark plug** (in gasoline engines) extends into the combustion chamber to ignite the mixture. The piston has **piston rings** that seal against the cylinder wall and control oil. A **connecting rod** links the piston to the crankshaft below, converting the up-down motion to rotation.
