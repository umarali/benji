I'll show you the internal structure of a jet engine with its key components.

A jet engine works by compressing air, mixing it with fuel and igniting it, then expelling the hot exhaust to create thrust. The main sections are:

The **fan** at the front pulls in massive amounts of air. Behind it, the **compressor** stages squeeze the air to high pressure. In the **combustion chamber**, fuel is sprayed and ignited, creating extremely hot expanding gases. These gases spin the **turbine**, which powers the compressor and fan through a central shaft. Finally, the hot exhaust blasts out the **nozzle** at the rear, producing thrust.
