I'll show you an airplane with its key parts and how they work together for flight.

An airplane flies by using its wings to generate lift as air flows over them. The engines provide thrust to move forward, while the tail controls pitch (nose up/down) and yaw (left/right), and the ailerons on the wings control roll (banking). The faster the plane moves, the more lift the wings create, allowing it to overcome gravity and stay airborne.
